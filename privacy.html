日语背单词 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 26 日

本应用「日语背单词」（下称“本应用”）由个人开发者郑启荣提供。我们重视你的隐私，本政策说明本应用如何处理信息。

我们收集哪些信息

本应用不收集、不上传、不与第三方共享任何个人信息。单词、学习进度、练习记录、设置和备份均只保存在你的设备本地。

设备上的数据

  • 你登记或导入的单词、读音、释义、音调、例句、标签和备注
  • 学习排程、复习日志、练习记录、自定义练习列表
  • 应用设置（如每日新词数、发音语速、书写判定方式、界面语言）

这些数据存放在本机 SQLite 数据库中。卸载应用会删除这些数据。你可以通过应用内的“备份 / 恢复”自行导出或导入 JSON 备份文件。

权限与系统功能

  • 文件访问：仅在你主动选择导入单词表或导出/恢复备份时，通过系统文件选择器读写你指定的文件。本应用不会在后台扫描相册或其它文件。
  • 语音合成：使用系统自带的文字转语音朗读日语或释义，不录音，不上传语音。

本应用不使用广告、分析 SDK、崩溃上报，也不使用 ATT 跟踪。

第三方内容

内置音调词典来自国立国語研究所 UniDic（BSD 许可），随应用分发，不涉及网络请求或用户数据。

儿童

本应用面向一般用户学习日语，不专门面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不收集儿童的个人信息。

政策更新

若本政策有重大变更，我们会更新本页面并调整生效日期。

联系我们

如对隐私有疑问，请通过 App Store 产品页的开发者联系方式，或发送邮件至 kenkindy@qq.com。

JP Vocab Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 October 2026

The app “JP Vocab” / “日语背单词” (the “App”) is provided by independent developer Qirong Zheng. This policy explains how the App handles information.

Information we collect

The App does not collect, upload, or share any personal information with us or with third parties. Words, study progress, practice records, settings, and backups stay on your device.

Data on the device

  • Words you add or import, including spelling, reading, meaning, pitch accent, examples, tags, and notes
  • Study schedule, review logs, practice records, and custom practice lists
  • App settings (daily new-word limit, speech rate, typing-quiz rules, interface language, and similar)

This data is stored in a local SQLite database. Uninstalling the App deletes it. You can export or restore a JSON backup from Settings whenever you choose.

Permissions and system features

  • Files: the system document picker is used only when you import a word list or export/restore a backup. The App does not scan your photo library or other files in the background.
  • Speech: the App uses the system text-to-speech voices. It does not record audio and does not upload speech.

The App does not include ads, analytics SDKs, crash reporting, or App Tracking Transparency tracking.

Third-party content

The bundled pitch-accent dictionary is derived from UniDic (National Institute for Japanese Language and Linguistics), distributed under the BSD license. It is shipped with the App and involves no network request or user data.

Children

The App is a general-audience Japanese learning tool. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not collect children’s personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes in a material way, we will update this page and the effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer contact on the App Store product page, or email kenkindy@qq.com.
