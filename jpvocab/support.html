来信时请尽量写明：设备型号、iOS 版本、问题现象，以及是否可以复现。我们会尽快回复。

常见问题

  • 数据在哪里？单词、进度、练习记录和设置都只保存在你的手机上，没有账号，也不会上传。
  • 如何备份？打开设置 → 数据 → 导出备份，保存 JSON 文件。换机或重装前请先导出。
  • 听不到发音？请确认系统已下载日语（ja-JP）语音，并检查设置里的语速和静音开关。听写/播报在静音模式下仍会出声。
  • 导入失败？支持 CSV / TXT。两列时视为「写法, 释义」。也可在第一行写表头：写法、读音、释义、音调、词性、标签。
  • 音调不准？音调来自 UniDic，仅供参考。登记时可改，同音假名（如 はし）可点候选切换。

FAQ

  • Where is my data? Words, progress, practice records, and settings stay on your phone. There is no account and nothing is uploaded.
  • How do I back up? Open Settings → Data → Export backup, and keep the JSON file. Export before you switch phones or reinstall.
  • No speech? Make sure a Japanese (ja-JP) system voice is installed, then check speech rate and the mute switch in Settings. Broadcast and dictation still speak when the mute switch is on.
  • Import failed? CSV and TXT are supported. Two columns are treated as spelling and meaning. You can also put a header row: spelling, reading, meaning, pitch, part of speech, tags.
  • Pitch looks wrong? Accent data comes from UniDic and is for reference. You can edit it when adding a word; homophones such as はし have tappable candidates.
